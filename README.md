# Executive-business-overview
## 📊 Executive Business Performance Dashboard

### Project Overview

The **Executive Business Performance Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence portfolio project designed to analyse sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, and revenue targets.

Built using fictional business data, the project demonstrates how raw transactional data can be transformed into meaningful insights that support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance through four analytical sections:

- **Executive Overview:** High-level KPIs covering total revenue, estimated profit, sales growth, order volume, and overall business performance.
- **Sales Performance:** Analysis of revenue trends, product performance, sales categories, and regional contributions.
- **Customer Analytics:** Insights into customer segmentation, purchasing patterns, and customer contributions to revenue.
- **Target Performance:** Comparison of actual sales against targets, highlighting achievement rates and performance variances.

### Key Features

- Interactive dashboard with year and region filters.
- KPI cards displaying critical business performance metrics.
- Visual analysis of revenue, profitability, and sales trends.
- Customer and product performance comparisons.
- Sales target tracking and variance analysis.
- Clean, professional dashboard design for executive reporting.

### Technologies & Skills Demonstrated

**Technologies:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, CSV, Power BI modelling concepts, and DAX documentation.

**Skills:** Data modelling, business intelligence, KPI development, data visualisation, sales analytics, performance monitoring, and dashboard design.

### Project Objective

To demonstrate how business intelligence and analytical techniques can help organisations monitor performance, identify growth opportunities, understand customer behaviour, and make informed strategic decisions.

**Note:** This portfolio project uses synthetic data and is presented as an interactive web dashboard.
